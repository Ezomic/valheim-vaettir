# Vaettir: a wild mushroom beside a new bed does not decide its rows.
#
# LHM-29, found in review before it shipped. The grid reads the rows off plants already in the
# ground, grown crops included. Thicket's seedlings grow into the wild prefabs themselves, so
# Pickable_Mushroom counted as a grown crop, and a mushroom is spaced exactly like a carrot. A
# lone wild mushroom within reach beat the shared grid one plant to none, and a bed started
# next to it took that mushroom's rows. A second bed next to a different mushroom took its rows.
# That is two patches of one field on two grids again.
#
# Now anything the world grows by itself is not counted. So: one mushroom stood on the ground
# beside you, and a carrot aimed next to it. The carrot must go on the shared grid, as it would
# on bare ground.
#
# This only proves something while a mushroom seedling and a carrot are spaced alike, which is
# 1m for both with Thicket's SpacingScale at 1 and carrots as they ripped before 1.0. If they
# differ the mushroom never counted and this passes for that reason instead. The carrot line in
# the report says the carrot's spacing, and the log names Pickable_Mushroom among the plants
# that grow wild.
#
# Leaves one mushroom standing, since `wipe` only takes down pieces. Pick it. Takes down every
# piece within 20m first. Stand on open ground.

wipe 20
wait 1

grow Pickable_Mushroom 1 3
wait 1

mod furrowtest forget
mod furrowtest plant sapling_carrot 0.5 0.5
printed shared grid yes
printed in line no
