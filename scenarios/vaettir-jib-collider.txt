# Vaettir: the jib's collision follows its mesh part by part (LHM-77).
#
# The collider is fifteen oriented boxes fitted by tools/jib/fit_col.py, one per physical part (two
# mast timbers, two buttress timbers, two deck braces, the deck, the hoist boom and its brace, the
# basket, two tiers of cage as an octagon, the prong crown). The first collider was six axis-aligned
# boxes measured off height bands; the mast leans, so its box bloated into invisible walls. Cords,
# cleats, rails and the prong tines carry no box on purpose.
#
# `hod bounds` reads the nearest standing jib and prints one summary line, then one `hod box` line
# per box with its part, centre, size and rotation in the jib's own space.
#
#   gap        lowest collider point minus the ground      top   highest point, minus the ground
#   meshtop    the mesh's highest point, minus the ground  topdiff   their difference
#   centreout  how far the farthest box centre lies outside the mesh's bounding box
#   footprint  the boxes' shadow on the ground, m2, against meshfootprint (measured off the mesh by
#              fit_col.py and written into hod_jib.col), as footratio
#
# What is asserted, each as its own flag so a failure names itself:
#   colliders=15            the box count, so a stray extra or a lost box is seen
#   floating=no buried=no   nothing starts more than 5cm above or below the ground
#   grounded=yes            the lowest point is within 5cm of the ground. The model is cut flat at
#                           the ground now (the old one was sunk 13cm on purpose, and there is no
#                           stone footing any more), so this holds and is asserted
#   tops=match              the collider top within 5cm of the mesh top (the prong crown)
#   tall=yes                a top between 2.5m and 4.5m, so a toy or a tower is caught
#   centres=inside          no box centre more than 5cm outside the mesh bounds: a box hanging in
#                           the air by itself is the thing this catches
#   footprint_ok=yes        the boxes' shadow at most 15 percent larger than the mesh's
#
# On flat ground only. The stage levels thirty metres, which is what makes `gap` the model's own and
# not a slope's. A slope is a hand check, and on one the flat-cut feet leave a gap on the downhill
# side, which is the price of sitting flush on level ground.
#
# MANUAL CHECKS, which this cannot do:
#  1. Walk up to a jib and round it. Want: you are stopped by the mast, the legs and the basket, and
#     not by empty air beside them.
#  2. Walk under the hoist boom, from the mast to the basket. Want: nothing stops you above 2.2m; the
#     basket stops you at its own width.
#  3. Stand a chest or a workbench against a leg. Want: it can touch the timber, not float away from
#     it.
#  4. Look at the hover and the placement ghost. Want: the ghost sits on the ground, not above it.

clear
give Hammer:1,Hoe:1
give FineWood:250,BronzeNails:40,IronNails:52,Chain:4,GroveHeartwood:6
god
ghost

biome Meadows
wait 3
stage
landed
mark

place hod_jib 6 0
wait 4
console hod bounds
printed standing=yes colliders=15 yes
printed floating=no buried=no yes
printed grounded=yes yes
printed tops=match tall=yes yes
printed centres=inside yes
printed footprint_ok=yes yes
printed hod box 0 part=mast_timber yes
printed hod box 14 part=prong_crown yes
printed hod box 15 no

landed
back
