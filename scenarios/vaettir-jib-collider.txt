# Vaettir: the jib's collision stands on the ground and reaches the top of its model (LHM-77).
#
# `hod bounds` reads the nearest standing jib: the union of its solid colliders against the ground
# under it, and its mesh against the same ground, in metres.
#
#   gap     collider bottom minus ground       top     collider top minus ground
#   meshtop mesh top minus ground              floating, buried, grounded, tops, tall   yes/no flags
#
# floating is a collider that starts more than 5cm ABOVE the ground, which lets a player walk under
# the footing. buried is one that starts more than 20cm below it. tops=match is the collider's top
# within 15cm of the mesh's, so a collider that stops short of the cage (the old single arm box did)
# or runs past it is caught. tall is a top between 2.5m and 4.5m, so a model scaled to a toy or to a
# tower is caught.
#
# THE FOOTING IS SUNK 13cm ON PURPOSE. The mesh and the footing box both reach -0.13 below the
# origin, so a jib on a slope has no gap under its downhill foot. That makes the strict reading,
# grounded (within 5cm either way), read no, and it is printed but NOT asserted: asserting it would
# fail on a deliberate design. If the jib should sit exactly on the ground the model and its .col
# move, not this file. What is asserted is the half that matters: nothing floats, nothing is buried
# deeper than the footing, and the top is where the model's top is.
#
# On flat ground only. The stage levels thirty metres, which is what makes `gap` the model's own and
# not a slope's. A slope is a hand check.
#
# MANUAL CHECKS, which this cannot do:
#  1. Walk up to a jib and round it. Want: you are stopped at the footing, the mast and the basket,
#     and not by empty air beside them.
#  2. Jump onto the footing. Want: you stand on it, you do not sink.
#  3. Build one on a slope. Want: no gap under the downhill foot.
#  4. Look at the hover and the placement ghost. Want: the ghost sits on the ground, not above it.

clear
give Hammer:1,Hoe:1
give FineWood:250,BronzeNails:40,IronNails:52,Chain:4,GroveHeartwood:6
god
ghost

biome Meadows
wait 3
stage
landed
mark

place hod_jib 6 0
wait 4
console hod bounds
printed standing=yes colliders=6 yes
printed floating=no buried=no yes
printed tops=match tall=yes yes

landed
back
