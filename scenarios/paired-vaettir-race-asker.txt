#! partner paired-vaettir-race-owner
# Vaettir, TWO CLIENTS - the side that has to ask, racing the side that does not.
#
# Nothing to press here. Devkit runs this on client B the moment paired-vaettir-race-owner.txt
# starts on client A, which builds everything, owns the chest and stocks it with exactly one
# bow's worth. Read that file's header for what the pair is for and how it is run.
#
# This client owns nothing. Its craft fires a withdrawal request that is in flight while the
# owner is spending out of the same chest locally, and the owner's clamp against live stock
# is the single line that decides whether this run produces one bow or two.
#
# THIS CLIENT WINS EVERY TIME, AND THAT IS NOT A FLAW - it is the design, seen from the
# outside. Six runs, six wins, and it took six to notice the pattern was not luck. The
# withdrawal is fired from a POSTFIX ON OnCraftPressed, so the material leaves the owner's
# chest within a frame or two of the press; the owner's own spend happens in DoCrafting, when
# the craft timer expires two seconds later. So the two payments are two seconds apart
# however close together the buttons are pressed, and this side is always first.
#
# What that makes this pair: proof that an owner whose chest has been emptied under it
# refuses its own craft rather than underpaying for one. Worth having, and not a race.
#
# The genuine simultaneous case - both payments in the same handful of frames - is
# paired-vaettir-race-asker-late.txt, which is this file plus a wait that lines this client's
# press up with the owner's DoCrafting instead of with its press.
#
# It stays wherever this character is until A's chest is stocked, then comes over with
# `partner`. Not before, because A stages its ground first, and a stage can move A.

clear
give Hammer:1,Hoe:1
god
ghost
sweep

# The signals this side waits for. It starts in the same second as A's file, and a `waitfor` on
# a key an abandoned run left set would return before A's own clearing got here. A key removed
# from a client is only gone here once the server's answer comes back, which is what the wait is
# for. A sets none of them until its build is standing, so this cannot clear a live one.
unkey rig_race_ready
unkey rig_race_go
unkey rig_a_dropped
wait 2

# Carried once so the material is known and the recipe appears in the panel, then thrown
# away. A single plank here and the craft is partly self-funded, which would let both sides
# succeed honestly and hide the thing being looked for.
give Wood:1,LeatherScraps:1
strip Wood,LeatherScraps
wait 1

waitfor rig_race_ready 300

# Beside A, two metres to its right and facing its way, short of the shed A built ahead of itself.
partner

# Walk to the bench BEFORE the race starts. A teleport is slow and would make this client
# late by seconds rather than by frames.
goto piece_workbench
wait 1
use piece_workbench
craftable Bow yes

key rig_race_set

# Held here until the owner fires, and then straight into it.
waitfor rig_race_go 120
craft Bow

wait 12

# Nothing is a good outcome. It means the owner won, which is one of the two right answers.
close
drop Bow
key rig_b_dropped

waitfor rig_a_dropped 120
wait 2

# One bow's worth of material, one bow. Two here is the bug.
dropped Bow 1

# This client's replica of a chest it does not own. Not authoritative - the owner asserts
# that - but a disagreement between the two is how a lost reply looks.
holds piece_chest_wood Wood 0
holds piece_chest_wood LeatherScraps 0
