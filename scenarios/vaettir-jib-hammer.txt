# Vaettir: the hammer is served by the chests in a jib's reach (LHM-76).
#
# A wood wall costs 2 wood. The chest holds 200 and the pack none, so every plank placed comes
# out of the chest, and the amounts are exact on purpose: "about right" cannot tell a spend from
# a free build. Then the pack is paid first and the chest only the rest, the piece is refused
# once the jib is gone, and a piece placed out of reach of any jib is refused too.
# The click path is driven by `build`, which presses the place button through the real
# Player.UpdatePlacement, not by calling the cost check.
# Not yet run in game.
clear
give Hammer:1,Hoe:1
give FineWood:250,BronzeNails:40,IronNails:52,Chain:4,GroveHeartwood:6
give Wood:100,Stone:40
god
ghost

# The Meadows, found rather than promised, because a suite run starts wherever the scenario
# before it left you. On 2026-09-28 that was the Deep North, inside the Queen's entrance
# utangard-queen-door-stays-sealed spawns, and ten Vaettir files failed on the place and not
# on the mod: tilling refused as snow too deep, and the entrance's own pieces, cut through by
# `wipe`, falling and dropping copper scrap. Already in the Meadows, this does not move you.
biome Meadows
wait 3

# Cleared and levelled where `biome` left you: every piece, drop, tree and rock within thirty
# metres taken down, and the ground levelled to the height under your feet. Devkit's automatic
# stage before the first step ran where the file before left you, which is not here when `biome`
# moved you. It replaces the `sweep` and `wipe 16` that stood here, doing both over more ground.
stage

key defeated_eikthyr

place piece_workbench 2 -3
wait 2
place hod_jib 4 2
wait 4
place piece_chest_wood 6 3
wait 2
claim piece_chest_wood
wait 2
stock piece_chest_wood Wood:200,Stone:200
strip Wood,Stone
wait 1
mark

# The menu's own test says yes, and a click builds it out of the chest alone.
buildable woodwall yes
tool Hammer
build woodwall 3 0 yes
wait 2
holds piece_chest_wood Wood 198
carrying Wood 0

# One wood in the pack and the rest in the chest: pack first, then chest. The pack ends at 0 and
# the chest gives 1, so 197 is left, not 196.
give Wood:1
wait 1
build woodwall 3 3 yes
wait 2
carrying Wood 0
holds piece_chest_wood Wood 197

# Out of every jib's reach the same wall is unbuildable: the workbench is out of range too, so
# the piece used is the workbench, which needs no station.
move 40 0
wait 2
buildable piece_workbench no
back
wait 1

# And with the jib gone, nothing in reach serves the hammer.
break hod_jib
wait 3
buildable woodwall no
build woodwall 3 -3 no

close
landed
back
