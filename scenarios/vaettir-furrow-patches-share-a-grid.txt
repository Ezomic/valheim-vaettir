# Vaettir: two patches of a field, planted apart and of different crops, are on one grid.
#
# LHM-29. Robbin: "when planting crops it sometimes looks like it's using a different grid for
# a certain patch than another". A patch started more than four metres from the last plant of
# the SAME crop used to start a grid of its own under the cursor, so a turnip patch beside a
# carrot patch, or a second carrot patch across a path, came out on its own rows.
#
# Now a patch on open ground goes on one grid shared by the whole world. The carrots here and
# the turnips seven metres off never see each other - that is further than the grid looks for
# neighbours - and must still line up.
#
# Carrots and turnips both ripped at one metre apart, so they can share a grid at all. That was
# before 1.0; the plant lines in the report say what the game gives now. The offsets assume
# that metre. With GridCell or Spacing set wider, two plants can round onto one spot.
#
# Assumes GridShared is on, which is the default. With it off the turnips start a grid of their
# own where they are aimed, and the last assertion fails - correctly.
#
# Takes down every piece within 20m first. Stand on open ground.

wipe 20
wait 1

mod furrowtest forget
mod furrowtest plant sapling_carrot 2 2
printed shared grid yes
mod furrowtest plant sapling_carrot 3 2
mod furrowtest plant sapling_carrot 2 3
mod furrowtest plant sapling_carrot 3 3
wait 1

# Seven metres off, and a different crop, after the grid has been made to forget.
mod furrowtest forget
mod furrowtest plant sapling_turnip 9.3 2.4
printed shared grid yes
mod furrowtest plant sapling_turnip 10.3 2.4
mod furrowtest plant sapling_turnip 9.3 3.4
wait 1

mod furrow check sapling_carrot,sapling_turnip 20
printed on one grid yes
