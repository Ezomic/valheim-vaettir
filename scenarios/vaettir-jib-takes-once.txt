# Vaettir: crafting takes from the chest exactly once, and exactly what it costs.
#
# The half that was never tested. Every assertion before this one asked whether a recipe
# READS as makeable; none of them made anything, which left the part that actually moves
# items untouched - and moving items is where a mod of this kind goes wrong. Counting a
# chest twice is a display bug. Taking from it twice is duplication.
#
# A Crude bow costs Wood 10 and LeatherScraps 8. The chest starts with exactly 20 and 16,
# which is two bows' worth, so after one craft it must hold 10 and 8 - and after two, none.
# Round numbers on purpose: "it went down by about the right amount" is not an assertion.
#
# Stand on flat ground with about ten metres clear, away from other posts and chests.

# Open the chest and shut it again before putting anything in. A freshly placed chest can
# have an OWNERLESS ZDO for a second or two, and Hod counts an ownerless chest for the
# display while refusing to spend out of it - correctly, because nobody may write to an
# ownerless ZDO and a removal that never reached it would come back on the next revision.
# So the panel says yes and the payment honestly says no, for about two seconds.
#
# A player never meets that window. A scenario places, stocks and crafts inside it, and the
# first run of this refused both crafts and read exactly like a bug in the mod.
use piece_chest_wood
close
wait 2

# Exactly two bows' worth, and nothing else in there.
stock piece_chest_wood Wood:20,LeatherScraps:16
stacks piece_chest_wood 2

# Your own pack holds none of it, so every plank spent has to come out of that chest.
strip Wood,LeatherScraps
wait 1

goto piece_workbench
wait 1
use piece_workbench
craftable Bow yes

craft Bow
wait 6

# One bow made: ten wood and eight scraps gone, and both stacks still there.
stacks piece_chest_wood 2

craft Bow
wait 6

# Two bows made: the chest is empty. Not "nearly", not "one stack left".
stacks piece_chest_wood 0

# And a third cannot be made out of nothing.
craftable Bow no

close
