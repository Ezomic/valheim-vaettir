# Vaettir: crafting takes from the chest exactly once, and exactly what it costs.
#
# The half that was never tested. Every assertion before this one asked whether a recipe
# READS as makeable; none of them made anything, which left the part that actually moves
# items untouched - and moving items is where a mod of this kind goes wrong. Counting a
# chest twice is a display bug. Taking from it twice is duplication.
#
# A Crude bow costs Wood 10 and LeatherScraps 8. The chest starts with exactly 20 and 16,
# which is two bows' worth, so after one craft it must hold 10 and 8 - and after two, none.
# Round numbers on purpose: "it went down by about the right amount" is not an assertion.
#
# Stand on flat ground with about ten metres clear, away from other posts and chests.

clear
give Hammer:1,Hoe:1
give FineWood:250,BronzeNails:40,IronNails:52,Chain:4,GroveHeartwood:6
# LeatherScraps:1 is not a typo and is not spare material. A recipe only appears in the
# crafting panel once the character has CARRIED every one of its materials at least once -
# Player.m_knownMaterial, filled by picking things up - so a character that has never held
# leather scraps cannot craft a bow however full the chest beside it is. It is stripped
# again below with the wood, so nothing here is self-funded.
give Wood:100,Stone:40,LeatherScraps:1
god
ghost

# The Meadows, found rather than promised, because a suite run starts wherever the scenario
# before it left you. On 2026-09-28 that was the Deep North, inside the Queen's entrance
# utangard-queen-door-stays-sealed spawns, and ten Vaettir files failed on the place and not
# on the mod: tilling refused as snow too deep, and the entrance's own pieces, cut through by
# `wipe`, falling and dropping copper scrap. Already in the Meadows, this does not move you.
biome Meadows
wait 3

sweep

wipe 16
wait 1

# Down onto the ground, since `wipe` can take the last file's floor out from under you and
# leave you hanging (0.6m up in 2.3.0's fourth run, and `flatten` refused), and remember the
# spot for the `back` at the end. See vaettir-jib-requirement-line.txt.
landed
mark

key defeated_eikthyr

# Round the shed's middle, not round your feet, so its back is level too.
flatten 4.4 0
wait 1

# The shed, so the bench can be crafted at. Captured from one built by hand.
place wood_beam 3.2 1.0 5 0.01
place wood_beam 3.4 -1.0 -174 0.00
place wood_floor 4.2 1.1 -174 0.02
place wood_floor 4.4 -0.9 -174 0.03
place wood_beam 4.1 2.1 -84 0.00
place wood_beam 4.5 -1.9 -84 0.03
place wood_beam 5.2 1.2 -174 0.00
place wood_beam 5.4 -0.8 -174 0.06
place piece_workbench 4.6 0.1 -174 0.10
place wood_pole2 3.1 2.0 5 1.04
place wood_pole2 3.5 -2.0 5 1.00
place woodwall 4.1 2.1 96 1.00
place woodwall 4.5 -1.9 -84 1.03
place woodwall 5.2 1.2 5 1.00
place woodwall 5.4 -0.8 5 1.06
place wood_pole2 5.1 2.2 5 1.01
place wood_pole2 5.5 -1.8 5 1.07
place wood_roof_ocorner_45 4.2 1.1 5 2.02
place wood_roof_ocorner_45 4.4 -0.9 -84 2.03
place wood_beam 4.1 2.1 95 2.00
place wood_beam 4.5 -1.9 -84 2.03
place wood_beam 5.2 1.2 5 2.00
place wood_beam 5.4 -0.8 5 2.06
place wood_beam_45 3.2 1.0 -174 3.01
place wood_beam_45 3.4 -1.0 6 3.00
wait 2

place stow_post 9 2
wait 6
place hod_jib 9 4
wait 4
place piece_chest_wood 7 3
wait 2

# Take ownership of the chest before putting anything in. A freshly placed chest can
# have an OWNERLESS ZDO for a second or two, and Hod counts an ownerless chest for the
# display while refusing to spend out of it - correctly, because nobody may write to an
# ownerless ZDO and a removal that never reached it would come back on the next revision.
# So the panel says yes and the payment honestly says no, for about two seconds.
#
# A player never meets that window. A scenario places, stocks and crafts inside it, and the
# first run of this refused both crafts and read exactly like a bug in the mod.
claim piece_chest_wood
wait 2

# Exactly two bows' worth, and nothing else in there.
stock piece_chest_wood Wood:20,LeatherScraps:16
stacks piece_chest_wood 2

# Your own pack holds none of it, so every plank spent has to come out of that chest.
strip Wood,LeatherScraps
wait 1

goto piece_workbench
wait 1
use piece_workbench
craftable Bow yes

craft Bow
wait 6

# One bow made: ten wood and eight scraps gone, and both stacks still there.
#
# The amounts rather than the stack count, because a stack count cannot tell this apart
# from nothing having happened at all - twenty wood and ten wood are both "one stack". The
# bow in the pack is the other half of the same question: a craft that is refused and a
# craft that happens for free both leave a chest looking plausible.
stacks piece_chest_wood 2
holds piece_chest_wood Wood 10
holds piece_chest_wood LeatherScraps 8
carrying Bow 1

craft Bow
wait 6

# Two bows made: the chest is empty. Not "nearly", not "one stack left".
stacks piece_chest_wood 0
holds piece_chest_wood Wood 0
holds piece_chest_wood LeatherScraps 0
carrying Bow 2

# And a third cannot be made out of nothing.
craftable Bow no

close

# Back to where this began, so the next file does not start on this shed's floor.
landed
back
