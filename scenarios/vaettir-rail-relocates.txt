# Vaettir: a shrink relocates before it drops, and drops nothing when it does not have to.
#
# The case a full post cannot test. Fill all 24, then take 12 out of the TOP rows, and what
# is left is twelve stacks sitting in the two rows that are about to stop existing, with
# twelve free slots above them. Break the rail and every one of them should move up.
#
# If this passes and vaettir-rail-spills also passes, relocation and spilling are both real.
# If this drops twelve, the shrink is throwing rows away rather than moving what fits.
#
# The long waits are PostSize's five second settle - see the note in vaettir-rail-spills.
#
# Stand on flat ground with about six metres clear in front of you, and NOT within twenty
# metres of another stowing post.

clear
give Hammer:1,Hoe:1
give FineWood:250,BronzeNails:40,IronNails:52,LeatherScraps:16,GroveHeartwood:6
god
ghost
sweep

# Take down whatever the last run left standing, so this spot can be used again
# instead of walking to fresh ground every time. It removes EVERYTHING built
# within 14m, not just what a scenario placed - do not run this near a real base.
wipe 14
wait 1

place stow_post 4 0
wait 6
place stow_rail 4 2
wait 4
grid stow_post 6x4

fill stow_post 24
thin stow_post 12
stacks stow_post 12

break stow_rail
wait 4

grid stow_post 6x2
stacks stow_post 12
ground 0
