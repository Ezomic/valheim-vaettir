# Vaettir: a plain post is 6x2 and stays that way through an unload.
#
# Plan steps 5 to 8. The dull one, and the one that matters most: this is the path that has
# actually destroyed things. ZNetScene tears a base down when you are far enough away and
# rebuilds it when you come back, and a post that guesses its size wrong on the way back in
# loads a grid of items into a smaller grid.
#
# The six second wait after placing is not padding. PostSize holds a post that has never
# recorded a size at its WIDEST for five seconds, deliberately, so a fresh post really is
# 6x4 for five seconds and only then becomes 6x2.
#
# Stand on flat ground with a few metres clear, and NOT near another stowing post.

clear
give Hammer:1,Hoe:1
give FineWood:250,BronzeNails:40,GroveHeartwood:6
god
ghost

# The Meadows, found rather than promised, because a suite run starts wherever the scenario
# before it left you. On 2026-09-28 that was the Deep North, inside the Queen's entrance
# utangard-queen-door-stays-sealed spawns, and ten Vaettir files failed on the place and not
# on the mod: tilling refused as snow too deep, and the entrance's own pieces, cut through by
# `wipe`, falling and dropping copper scrap. Already in the Meadows, this does not move you.
biome Meadows
wait 3

sweep

wipe 14
wait 1

place stow_post 4 0
wait 6
grid stow_post 6x2

fill stow_post 12
stacks stow_post 12

# Out far enough that the base unloads, then back to the spot you left, with `back` and not
# with `move -250`. `move` sets you down on the highest thing under its target, so the way
# back is only the way out while nothing stands over where you stood. On 2026-09-28 something
# did: the return put you on top of the Queen's entrance, 35m above the post, and `grid` and
# `stacks` look 20m round you, so both read "no stow_post nearby" with the post standing
# there full. `back` goes to the spot `mark` took.
mark
move 250
wait 3
back
wait 3

grid stow_post 6x2
stacks stow_post 12
ground 0
