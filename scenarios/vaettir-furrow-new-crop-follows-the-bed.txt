# Vaettir: a turnip planted beside a carrot bed continues the carrots' rows.
#
# LHM-29. The grid used to look only for plants of the SAME crop, so the first turnip beside a
# carrot bed found none and started a grid of its own where it was aimed. That is the other way
# one field came out on two grids.
#
# The carrot bed is laid with `furrow free`, exactly where each is aimed and on no grid: a bed
# from before this fix, or one planted by hand with Shift. It is square with itself, a metre
# apart, and nowhere in particular against the shared grid. The turnips must follow the bed, not
# the shared grid.
#
# The vote is asserted as well as the result. "4 plants in line" says all four carrots were
# counted and agreed, rather than the turnip happening to land in line with one of them.
#
# The offsets assume carrots and turnips are spaced one metre apart, which is what they ripped
# at before 1.0. Takes down every piece within 20m first. Stand on open ground.

wipe 20
wait 1

mod furrow forget
mod furrow free sapling_carrot 2.37 2.21
mod furrow free sapling_carrot 3.37 2.21
mod furrow free sapling_carrot 2.37 3.21
mod furrow free sapling_carrot 3.37 3.21
wait 1

mod furrow plant sapling_turnip 4.3 2.3
printed 4 plants in line yes
mod furrow plant sapling_turnip 4.4 3.1
printed plants already there yes
wait 1

mod furrow check sapling_carrot,sapling_turnip 10
printed on one grid yes
