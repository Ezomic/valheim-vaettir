# Vaettir: turning the grid beside a bed does not turn the bed's rows.
#
# LHM-29, found in review; this is not what Robbin reported. The grid used to line a new plant up
# at your own angle only, so after a turn the bed beside you counted for nothing and the next
# plant started turned rows around one of its plants. The wheel turns the grid whenever a plant is
# selected, so that is easy to do without meaning to. Now two or more plants in line keep their
# own angle, and your angle decides open ground, a lone plant and the pin.
#
# So: a bed of four carrots, one notch of turn, a carrot beside the bed, and then a pair of
# carrots on open ground. The carrot beside the bed must land in the bed's rows. The pair must
# land on the TURNED grid, which is what proves the turn did anything at all.
#
# Every carrot goes in with `build`: the cultivator, the ghost, Furrow's snap and the place
# button. The turn is `mod furrowtest turn 1`, the same call the wheel makes, because a step
# cannot scroll. It writes GridAngle to your config the way a scroll does, and `turn -1` puts it
# back before the last check. The grid is NOT made to forget after the turn: it goes on holding
# the bed it was planting, now at the turned angle, which is exactly where a player is after a
# scroll. `mod furrowtest forget` runs once, at the start, to lift a pin and empty what the grid
# is holding, the state of a freshly started game. `free Oak_Sapling 0 12` and `face` turn you
# along the grid's columns, so each build's note says how far the grid moved that carrot in rows.
#
# What passes: the bed "on one grid" with 4; the bed with the carrot beside it "on one grid" with
# 5; the pair "on one grid"; and all seven "on 2 grids (5 + 2)", the bed at your old angle and the
# pair at the new one.
# What a broken build reads: a carrot that took your angle beside the bed reads "on 2 grids
# (4 + 1)" at the second check. A turn that did nothing puts the pair on the bed's grid, and the
# last check reads "on one grid" with 7.
#
# Needs: singleplayer, level ground in the Meadows with twenty metres clear. GridEnabled and
# GridShared on, GridCell 0, Sowing off, and a GridTurnStep that is not 0 or a multiple of 90.
# Farming is set to 50 and put back. If you stop it between the two turns, your grid is left one
# notch round: scroll it back once with the cultivator out and a plant selected.
#
# Takes down every piece within 20m first. Leaves seven carrots, the marker oak and the tilled
# ground; the next run's wipe takes the plants and the oak.

clear
give Cultivator:1,CarrotSeeds:10
skill Farming 50

# The Meadows, found rather than promised, because a suite run starts wherever the scenario
# before it left you. On 2026-09-28 that was the Deep North, inside the Queen's entrance
# utangard-queen-door-stays-sealed spawns, and ten Vaettir files failed on the place and not
# on the mod: tilling refused as snow too deep, and the entrance's own pieces, cut through by
# `wipe`, falling and dropping copper scrap. Already in the Meadows, this does not move you.
biome Meadows
wait 3

wipe 20
wait 1

mod furrowtest forget
mod furrowtest free Oak_Sapling 0 12
printed off any grid yes
wait 1
face Oak_Sapling 15
wait 1
tool Cultivator

# A second after every tilling swing. `build` reads the ground's height the moment it starts,
# and a swing's new ground only reaches the terrain's collider at the end of the frame it lands
# in, which is the frame Devkit starts the next step in. Without the wait the next `build`
# aims at the ground as it stood before the swing and fails "could not put the placement ray
# on the spot". See vaettir-furrow-patches-share-a-grid.
build cultivate_v2 3.4 -1.75
wait 1
build cultivate_v2 3.4 0
wait 1
build cultivate_v2 1.65 -1.75
wait 1
build cultivate_v2 1.65 0
wait 1
build cultivate_v2 2.6 -3.3
wait 4


# ---- the bed, far row first, on open ground and so on the shared grid

build sapling_carrot 3.4 -1.75
build sapling_carrot 3.4 0
build sapling_carrot 1.65 -1.75
build sapling_carrot 1.65 0
wait 1

mod furrow check sapling_carrot 6
printed furrow check: 4 yes
printed on one grid yes


# ---- one notch, the way the wheel turns it

mod furrowtest turn 1
printed grid at yes
wait 3

# Beside the bed on its left, within reach of all four. The bed's rows must beat your new angle.
build sapling_carrot 2.6 -3.3
wait 1

mod furrow check sapling_carrot 6
printed furrow check: 5 yes
printed on one grid yes


# ---- open ground, more than five metres from anything planted: your turned angle decides

move 0.5 7.5
build cultivate_v2 3.4 -1.75
wait 1
build cultivate_v2 3.4 0
wait 1
build sapling_carrot 3.4 -1.75
build sapling_carrot 3.4 0
wait 1

mod furrow check sapling_carrot 5
printed furrow check: 2 yes
printed on one grid yes

# Back to the angle you started with, before anything else can fail.
mod furrowtest turn -1
printed grid at yes

# All seven: the bed and its neighbour on one grid, the pair on another.
mod furrow check sapling_carrot 13
printed furrow check: 7 yes
printed rows 1m apart yes
printed on 2 grids (5 + 2) yes

skill Farming back
