# Vaettir: one plant put down off the grid does not pull the next one out of the bed.
#
# LHM-29. When the grid lost track of a bed it took the NEAREST plant as the new start of its
# rows. If that one had been planted by hand, off the grid, everything planted after it
# followed it instead of the bed - which reads in game as the grid changing for no reason
# partway through a field. The fix counts how many plants agree on each set of rows and goes
# with the most.
#
# So: a bed of four onions, a fifth planted by hand half a row off between them, and then an
# onion aimed right beside that stray. It must land in the bed's rows. The check prints the
# grids largest first, so "5 + 1" is the bed with the new onion in it and the stray alone,
# and "4 + 2" would be the new onion having followed the stray.
#
# The bed is laid with `furrowtest free` so its position against the stray is fixed and cannot
# depend on where the shared world grid happens to fall around you.
#
# The offsets assume onions are spaced one metre apart, which is what they ripped at before
# 1.0. Takes down every piece within 20m first.

wipe 20
wait 1

mod furrowtest forget
mod furrowtest free sapling_onion 2.37 2.21
mod furrowtest free sapling_onion 3.37 2.21
mod furrowtest free sapling_onion 4.37 2.21
mod furrowtest free sapling_onion 2.37 3.21

# The stray, half a row off both ways.
mod furrowtest free sapling_onion 3.82 3.66
wait 1

mod furrowtest forget
mod furrowtest plant sapling_onion 4.1 3.5
printed 4 plants in line yes
wait 1

mod furrow check sapling_onion 10
printed on 2 grids (5 + 1) yes
