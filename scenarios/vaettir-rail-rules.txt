# Vaettir: which rails count, and which are just furniture.
#
# Plan steps 17 and 18. A rail feeds the post it stands beside, one rail per post, within
# UpgradeRange - and a rail that does none of those things says so when you look at it
# rather than sitting there looking identical to one that works.
#
# The hover text is the only place any of this is reported, which is why it is asserted.
#
# Stand on flat ground with about ten metres clear, away from other posts.

clear
give Hammer:1,Hoe:1
give FineWood:250,BronzeNails:40,IronNails:52,LeatherScraps:40,GroveHeartwood:6
god
ghost
sweep

wipe 16
wait 1

place stow_post 4 0
wait 6
grid stow_post 6x2

# One rail, in range. The post grows and the rail says what it is feeding.
place stow_rail 4 2
wait 4
grid stow_post 6x4
hover stow_rail feeding

# A second rail beside the same post adds nothing, and admits it. Placed NEARER to you
# than the first, because hover reads the closest one by that name and the two were
# equidistant - so the assertion was quietly being made about the working rail.
place stow_rail 2 0
wait 3
hover stow_rail adds_nothing
grid stow_post 6x4

break stow_rail
wait 2

# A rail further away than UpgradeRange, which is 5m, feeds nothing at all.
place stow_rail 12 0
wait 3
grid stow_post 6x4
