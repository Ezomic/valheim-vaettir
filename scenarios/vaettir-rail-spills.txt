# Vaettir: a full post loses its rail and spills what will not fit.
#
# This is steps 14, 15, 19 and 20 of the hand-written plan, as one keypress.
#
# The post is 6x2 alone and 6x4 with a rail, which is a reinforced chest. Breaking the rail
# takes it back to 6x2: twelve stacks stay, twelve land on the grass - the same thing that
# happens when you break a chest.
#
# WHY THE WAITS ARE SO LONG. PostSize holds a post that has never recorded a size at its
# WIDEST for the first five seconds after it wakes (SettleSeconds), on purpose - a post that
# guessed small and guessed wrong would truncate a grid it had not finished reading. So a
# freshly placed post really is 6x4 for five seconds and only then becomes 6x2, and asking
# before that is asking the wrong question. The first run of this scenario waited 2 and
# failed on exactly that.
#
# Stand on flat ground with about six metres clear in front of you, and NOT within twenty
# metres of another stowing post - the steps find pieces by name and take the nearest.

clear
give Hammer:1,Hoe:1
give FineWood:250,BronzeNails:40,IronNails:52,LeatherScraps:16,GroveHeartwood:6
god
ghost

# Anything already lying about would be counted as spill later.
sweep

# Take down whatever the last run left standing, so this spot can be used again
# instead of walking to fresh ground every time. It removes EVERYTHING built
# within 14m, not just what a scenario placed - do not run this near a real base.
wipe 14
wait 1

place stow_post 4 0
wait 6
grid stow_post 6x2

place stow_rail 4 2
wait 4
grid stow_post 6x4

fill stow_post 24
stacks stow_post 24

break stow_rail
wait 4

grid stow_post 6x2
stacks stow_post 12
ground 12
