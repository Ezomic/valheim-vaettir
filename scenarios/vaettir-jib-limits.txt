# Vaettir: what the hod jib refuses to do (LHM-76, LHM-77).
#
# Until LHM-76 the jib served a bench and deliberately NOT the hammer, and this file asserted that
# refusal. The hammer is served now (vaettir-jib-hammer.txt), so what is left is the other side of
# the same line: the jib pays for a piece only out of a chest its reach holds, and only while it
# stands. Each refusal has a positive that differs in one thing, so none of them can be a missing
# bench or an empty pack:
#  1. A chest 26m from the jib (outside its 20m circle) holds all the wood. The jib stands, the
#     player is in its reach, the gate is open. `hod build` reads scope=open chests=0, and the wall
#     is unbuildable. A second chest 4m from the jib is then stocked, and the same wall is buildable:
#     the first chest was refused for where it stands, not for the wall or the pack.
#  2. The jib comes down with that chest in reach and full: scope=shut, unbuildable. Wood in the pack
#     turns it buildable, so the refusal was the jib.
#
# `build ... no` is not used for the refused click: it passes only when the SPOT was refused, and the
# refusal here is want of materials. `buildable` is the same Player.HaveRequirements test the click
# makes, and the chest counts are checked unchanged.
#
# Geometry, metres forward/right of the mark: jib (-8, 0); far chest (-8, 26), 26m from the jib and
# 26m from the mark; near chest (-8, 4). The bench is at (2, -3), 3.6m from the mark, in range of the
# wall's station test. Everything is inside the 30m the stage levels.
#
# MANUAL CHECKS, which this cannot do:
#  1. Look at a jib with the Hammer out. Want: its ring on the ground, 20m across.
#  2. Stand in the ring with the chest just outside it. Want: the wall is greyed.
# The stage takes down EVERYTHING built within thirty metres and the world's own trees and rocks for
# good, so run it in a test world and never near a real base.

clear
give Hammer:1,Hoe:1
give FineWood:250,BronzeNails:40,IronNails:52,Chain:4,GroveHeartwood:6
give Wood:100,Stone:40
god
ghost

biome Meadows
wait 3
stage
landed
mark

mod hodbuild on
unkey defeated_eikthyr
wait 1
key defeated_eikthyr
wait 2

console hod cost woodwall
printed lines=1 Wood=2 end yes
console hod build
printed setting=on enabled=on shut=no yields=none yes
printed gate=open yes

# A wall needs a workbench in range before it can be built at all. Without one `buildable woodwall no`
# passes for the wrong reason, which is the trap the crafting steps fell into twice.
place piece_workbench 2 -3
wait 2
place hod_jib -8 0
wait 4

# ---- 1. the full chest outside the circle
place piece_chest_wood -8 26
wait 3
claim piece_chest_wood
wait 1
stock piece_chest_wood Wood:20 30
strip Wood
wait 2
console hod build
printed scope=open chests=0 gate=open yes
buildable woodwall no
holds piece_chest_wood Wood 20 30

# The near chest, stocked: the same wall is buildable, and it is the near chest that pays.
place piece_chest_wood -8 4
wait 3
claim piece_chest_wood
wait 1
console hod build
printed scope=open chests=1 yes
stock piece_chest_wood Wood:20 12
wait 1
buildable woodwall yes
holds piece_chest_wood Wood 20 30

# ---- 2. the jib gone, both chests full
break hod_jib 20
wait 3
console hod build
printed scope=shut chests=0 yes
buildable woodwall no
give Wood:2
wait 1
buildable woodwall yes
strip Wood
wait 1
buildable woodwall no

landed
back
